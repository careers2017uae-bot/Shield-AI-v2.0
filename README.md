# Shield-AI-v2.0
SHIELD AI — Child Online Safety Platform (MVP)

A privacy-first, platform-independent child-safety screening prototype built forincubator and investor demonstration. SHIELD AI detects potential safety signalsin conversations, explains why something was flagged, and supports a humanreview process — without surveillance, without scraping, and without connectingto any real messaging platform.

    Demo environment. This prototype uses synthetic conversations only. Itdoes not connect to, scrape, or monitor WhatsApp or any other messagingplatform. It never accesses a real child's account.
